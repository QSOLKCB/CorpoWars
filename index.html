Spend your 6,000 starting credits in Research & Loadout before deploying. Purchases persist; mission rewards are paid once. Medical and body augmentation research applies to all four agents. Panic makes visible enemies retreat without firing, then recharges. AUG conversions cost a reload. Buildings block movement and gunfire. Selected markers remain visible through roofs. The minimap shows only detected enemies. Patches heal selected agents; EMP reaches seven tiles. Abilities require live time. On touchscreens, tap to command and use the on-screen controls.

New contracts: hold the Copperline transmitter while clearing contesting security; release and protect the Cold Storage courier; beat the Obsidian lockdown clock after cutting the relay. Select agents and press C or ESCORT COURIER to follow the witness automatically. Ground, attack and stop orders cancel escort duty. Bulwarks have heavy armour; marksmen shoot farther. Optional medals reward all four agents surviving, using no patches/EMP/panic, and finishing under the contract par. Medals carry no cash reward.
